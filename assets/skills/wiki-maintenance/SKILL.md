# wiki-maintenance

Choose page ownership from topic scope, not a convenient filename. Search similar entities and
concepts first. Before a move, enumerate inbound links and update them plus index entries in
the same change. Keep raw evidence and source URLs intact. Split long pages at coherent topics,
preserving context and reciprocal links. A relocation is not evidence of new research.

Available deterministic helpers (inspect arguments and scope before using):
- [scripts/build-orphan-sed.py](scripts/build-orphan-sed.py)
- [scripts/misplaced-entries-detection.py](scripts/misplaced-entries-detection.py)
