# wiki-concept-from-research

Read the paper or complete technical source, distinguish the proposed mechanism from claimed
results and limitations. Search existing terminology and broader/narrower concepts. New pages
must meet SCHEMA.md thresholds; otherwise enrich an existing one. Record paper versions,
benchmark conditions and uncertainty. Cross-link an entity page only when the relation is real.
